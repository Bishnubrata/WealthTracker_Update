# 🚀 Wealth Tracker

> A comprehensive, offline-first personal finance and productivity application designed to manage your money, plan goals, and track ideas in one secure, localized environment.

## 📌 Feature Glance

| Module | Primary Function | Key Capabilities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 📱 **Dashboard** | Transaction Logging | Voice-to-text, receipt uploads, recurring entries, backups |
| 📜 **Timeline** | Financial History | Chronological ledger, AI smart search, custom date filters |
| 📊 **Charts** | Analytics & Reporting | Interactive charts, timeframe toggles, PDF/Excel data export |
| ✅ **Plans** | Task Management | Priority tagging, status tracking, recurring bills |
| 🧮 **Rates** | Financial Toolkit | In-app number crunching and interest/tax calculators |
| 📝 **Notes** | Rich-Text Journal | Image attachments, color-coding, HTML sharing |
| ⚙️ **System** | Core Architecture | 100% offline SQLite database, Biometric lock, Dark Mode |

---

## 📱 Dashboard | The Command Center

*Log and manage your daily financial activities with minimal friction.*

* **Quick Logging:** Seamlessly input Income, Expenses, and Investments.
* **Smart Categorization:** Automatically categorize spending and income streams.
* **Rich Attachments:** Snap receipt photos or upload from your gallery (includes a 1-click quick remove function).
* **Voice-to-Text Input:** Dictate transaction remarks hands-free via the microphone integration.
* **Recurring Transactions:** Automate repetitive financial entries on a monthly or yearly basis.
* **Cloud Backup & Restore:** Manually export your complete SQLite database and images as a `.zip` file for safekeeping, or restore a previous backup.
* **In-App Auto Updater:** Fetch and install new app updates directly from GitHub with a live progress bar.

## 📜 Timeline | Financial History

*A chronological, easily searchable view of your financial life.*

* **Visual Ledger:** Scroll through past transactions with intuitive color-coding (🟢 Green for Income, 🔴 Red for Expenses).
* **AI Smart Search:** Leverage natural language processing to locate specific transactions instantly.
* **Custom Date Filters:** Isolate timeline data by selecting specific, custom date ranges.

## 📊 Charts | Analytics & Reporting

*Visualize your wealth and spending habits to make informed decisions.*

* **Interactive Visualizations:** Dynamic Pie and Bar charts breaking down your financial distribution.
* **Dynamic Timeframes:** Toggle between Day, Month, and Year perspectives to track spending trends over time.
* **Category Filters:** Isolate visual data exclusively by Income, Expenses, or Investments.
* **Export to Excel:** Export all transactions, plans, and notes into a fully formatted `.xlsx` spreadsheet.
* **Shareable Reports:** Generate comprehensive financial reports (Summary & Details) and instantly share them via HTML or PDF.

## ✅ Plans | Task & Goal Manager

*Manage financial goals, upcoming bills, and actionable to-do lists.*

* **Structured Tracking:** Schedule plans with precise dates, times, and detailed descriptions.
* **Prioritization:** Assign color-coded priority levels (High, Medium, Low) to keep focus on what matters.
* **Status Tracking:** Monitor progress by marking tasks as *New*, *In Progress*, or *Completed*.
* **Automation:** Set recurring schedules for ongoing bills or long-term financial goals.
* **Workspace Management:** Instantly sweep and delete all completed plans to maintain a tidy workspace.

## 🧮 Rates | Financial Calculators

*Your built-in financial toolkit.*

* **In-App Calculations:** Quickly crunch numbers, project investments, and calculate interest without switching applications.

## 📝 Notes | Rich-Text Journaling

*A dedicated space for financial thoughts, ideas, and journaling.*

* **Rich Media Support:** Jot down freeform text and attach multiple images directly within a single note.
* **Visual Organization:** Pin critical notes to the top and assign custom background colors for rapid visual sorting.
* **Export & Share:** Distribute your formatted notes (including text and images) as standalone HTML webpages.

## ⚙️ System-Wide Architecture

*Built from the ground up for privacy, speed, and security.*

* **100% Offline & Private:** Zero third-party server tracking. All financial data is stored locally on your device within a secure SQLite database.
* **Adaptive UI:** Fully responsive Light and Dark theme integration based on native device settings.
* **Biometric Security:** Enterprise-grade protection using your device's native fingerprint or facial recognition scanner.
