🚀 Wealth Tracker - Complete Feature Overview
Wealth Tracker is a comprehensive, offline-first personal finance and productivity application designed to help you manage your money, plan your goals, and track your ideas all in one place.

📱 Tab 1: Dashboard (Add Transactions)
The command center for logging your daily financial activities.

Quick Log: Easily add Income, Expenses, and Investments.
Smart Categories: Automatically categorizes your spending and income.
Image Attachments: Snap a picture of a receipt or choose from your gallery and attach it to a transaction. (Includes a quick red 'X' to instantly remove attachments).
Voice-to-Text Input: Use the microphone icon to dictate your transaction remarks hands-free.
Recurring Transactions: Set transactions to repeat Monthly or Yearly automatically.
In-App Auto Updater: (Top right icon) Check for new app updates directly from GitHub and install them seamlessly with a live progress bar.
Cloud Sync / Backup: (Top right icon) Manually export your entire database and images as a .zip file for safekeeping, or restore from a previous backup.
📜 Tab 2: Timeline (History)
A chronological view of your financial life.

Transaction History: Scroll through all past transactions with clear color-coding (Green for Income, Red for Expenses).
AI Smart Search: (Top right icon) Use natural language search to find specific transactions quickly.
Custom Date Filters: (Top right icon) Filter your timeline to only show transactions from a specific custom date range.
📊 Tab 3: Charts (Analytics)
Visualize your wealth and spending habits beautifully.

Interactive Charts: View dynamic Pie Charts and Bar Charts breaking down your finances.
Dynamic Timeframes: Switch between Day, Month, and Year views to see how your spending changes over time.
Category Filters: Filter charts to exclusively show Income, Expenses, or Investments.
Share Reports: (Top right icon) Instantly generate a comprehensive financial report (Summary & Details) and share it via HTML or PDF.
Export Data: (Top right icon) Export all your transactions, plans, and notes into a fully formatted Excel (.xlsx) spreadsheet.
✅ Tab 4: Plans (Task Manager)
Manage your financial goals, bills, and to-do lists.

Task Creation: Add structured plans with dates, times, and detailed descriptions.
Priority Levels: Assign priorities (High, Medium, Low) and color codes to your tasks.
Status Tracking: Mark tasks as New, In Progress, or Completed.
Recurring Plans: Set bills or goals to repeat automatically.
Clear Completed: (Top right icon) Quickly sweep away and delete all finished plans to keep your list tidy.
🧮 Tab 5: Rates (Calculators)
Your built-in financial toolkit.

Financial Calculators: (Depends on your exact implementation, usually includes interest, tax, or investment calculators).
Quickly crunch numbers without leaving the app.
📝 Tab 6: Notes
A rich-text notepad for your financial thoughts, ideas, and journal entries.

Quick Notes: Jot down freeform text ideas.
Image Support: Attach multiple images directly inside a note.
Pinning System: Pin important notes to the top of your list.
Color Coding: Assign custom background colors to your notes for easy visual organization.
Share as HTML: Export and share your beautiful notes (including text and images) as an HTML webpage.
⚙️ System-Wide Features
100% Offline & Private: Your financial data never touches a 3rd party server. Everything is stored locally on your device in a secure SQLite database.
Light / Dark Mode: Fully responsive theme support based on your device settings.
Biometric Security: (If enabled) Lock the app behind your phone's fingerprint or face scanner.
